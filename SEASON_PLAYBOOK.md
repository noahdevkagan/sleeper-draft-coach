## FAAB rules (the #1 leak)

1. **Hard cap: $30 on any single bid before Week 8.** No exceptions. The Trey Benson all-in ($100, W3, returned 35 pts) and the pattern of being broke before byes is the single most expensive habit in the data.
2. **Reserve $35–40 for Weeks 10–14.** ericff and juiceeJR win this league by buying the playoff push when everyone else is at $0. Copy it. Only ericff will have money to compete with you.
3. **Never bid $0 as a default. Bid $2–3.** Herbert's 182 points cost antonshanahan exactly $3. $0 claims lose every contested add for the price of a coffee.
4. **Two-week hold rule:** anyone you spend a claim on stays rostered 2 weeks minimum. Kills the churn reflex (83 adds / 61 claims in 2025, 8 QBs, 8 TEs started).

## Roster philosophy

- **QBs and TEs are free.** Jordan Love, Stroud, Mayfield, Purdy, Maye, Herbert, Stafford — every year's best QBs came off waivers. Never spend real FAAB on a QB; stream with $2–3 bids.
- **WRs are abundant** (Pittman, Diggs, Wan'Dale, Wilson, Shakir all 100+ pts off waivers in 2025). **RBs are not.** Spend FAAB on RBs, patience on WRs.
- **Don't cut drafted QBs in Week 1.** (Herbert, 2025. Never again.)

## v10.3 (Sep 25, 2026): QB, TE, DEF, K rules from a 2023-25 backtest

Data: Sleeper weekly stats 2023-25, Sleeper preseason ADP, ESPN closing spread and total for all 816 games.

**QB (why Maye at pick 47 was wrong)**
- Weekly free-agent QB (best Sleeper-projected undrafted QB) scored 21.5/wk in 2024 and 20.0/wk in 2025. The 8th-best drafted QB scored 17.5 and 18.1.
- Season points vs that free QB: R3-4 Allen +52 / +57. Other R3-4 QBs -51 to -26. R5-8 QBs averaged -15 (2024) and -17 (2025). R9-12 QBs -54 / -17.
- Rule: a QB goes before the late rounds only if his Vegas season projection beats QB2 by 30+ (Allen 2026: +43). Otherwise no QB until R12 of 16.

**TE (why McBride over Bowers)**
- The engine had them tied (Vegas 182 vs 180). Tiebreak now = prior-year PPR ppg: for TEs it predicted this-year ppg beyond ADP (r 0.33, n 25). 2026 case: McBride 18.9 vs Bowers 14.7.
- Games missed the prior year did NOT predict games missed (r -0.08, top-60 ADP). Do not fade players for injury history alone.

**DEF**
- ADP predicted nothing (r -0.04, -0.02). Last year's ppg barely (r 0.19, 0.39). Draft DEF in the last two picks by week-1 Vegas line.
- Weekly: opponent implied total is the best signal (r 0.32; Sleeper projection 0.29; season average 0.09). DEF pts = 17.34 - 0.462 x opp implied. Opp implied under 17: 9.8 pts. 24.5+: 5.1 pts.
- Season sim (8 teams, others hold 7 DEFs): hold one DEF 136 pts; hold and swap when opp implied is 22+ 155 pts with ~5 adds; stream every week 153 with ~15 adds. Rule: hold, swap only at 22+ and only if the free DEF is 2+ better.

**K (We Dem Boyz inverted scoring)**
- Scoring confirmed from league settings and Week 1-2 results: made FG 0, XP made 0. Missed FG = 3 + distance bonus (0-39: 6, 40-49: 5, 50+: 4). Missed XP 5.
- League average about 1.9 pts/game. Nothing predicts it: YoY r -0.04 (2024), +0.44 (2025); weekly team implied total r 0.04; indoor 1.72 vs outdoor 1.89.
- Rule: last pick, pick any starting kicker (engine lists last year's miss pts/g), never stream or spend a move on K.
